         AMENDED AND RESTATED CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of December 31, 2003
among RADIOLOGIX, INC., a Delaware corporation, as Borrower, the financial

                                    RECITALS:

         WHEREAS, Borrower, the other lenders party thereto (the "ORIGINAL
LENDERS") and GE Capital, as agent for the Original Lenders, are party to that
certain Amended and Restated Credit Agreement, dated December 12, 2001 (as
amended, the "ORIGINAL CREDIT AGREEMENT"); and

         WHEREAS, Borrower, GE Capital and the Original Lenders desire to
continue and to (i) amend and restate the Original Credit Agreement in its
entirety with this Agreement and (ii) amend and restate certain other Loan
Documents executed in connection with the Original Credit Agreement in
connection herewith; and

         NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and the agreements,
provisions and covenants herein contained, Borrower, Lenders and Agent hereby
agree as follows:

more of any class of voting securities of such Person or to direct or cause the
